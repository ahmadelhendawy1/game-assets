# Image prompts for every game (ChatGPT)

How to use:
1. Paste the right **style lock** first, then one prompt. One image per message.
2. If ChatGPT gives a white or checkered background instead of a transparent one, reply: "Same image, transparent background, PNG".
3. Upload the finished images in the Claude chat and say which game they are for. Claude cuts them, rigs and animates them, makes them seamless, compresses them and publishes the pack here.
